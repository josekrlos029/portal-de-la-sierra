FAQ

Questions buyers ask us

How much does a lot cost at Portal de la Sierra?

Homesites start at COP 250,000 per m² (≈ US$79/m², about US$7.35 per sq ft), so an 800 m² lot starts at COP 200,000,000 (≈ US$63,300). Dollar figures are estimates at COP 3,160 per US$; final pricing, payment options and availability are confirmed by Núñez Anaya Inmobiliaria.

How big are the lots?

There are 27 homesites: 25 of 800 m² (≈ 8,611 sq ft, about 0.2 acres), plus Lot 17 at 847 m² and Lot 18 at 939 m². The whole property covers 27,333 m² (≈ 6.75 acres).

Where is Portal de la Sierra located?

On the Valledupar – Sabana Crespo road, on the outskirts of Valledupar in the Cesar department of northern Colombia, in the foothills of the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta. The site sits 276–288 m (905–945 ft) above sea level.

Is it a gated community?

Yes. It is a private, gated country community with a 24/7 guard house, a security booth and a vehicle access gate.

What amenities are included?

A shared amenity area with a swimming pool, hot tub, palm-thatched kiosk and green spaces, plus an internal road that fronts every lot.

What can I build on an 800 m² lot?

There is room for a single-family country home with a garden and a pool — the gallery shows a single-story reference home. Ask your advisor for the community's building guidelines.

Can foreigners buy property in Colombia?

Yes. Foreigners and Colombians living abroad can buy real estate in Colombia with the same property rights as locals. Funds sent from abroad should come in through the official foreign-exchange market so the investment can be registered with Colombia's central bank (Banco de la República). This is general information — please confirm the details with a Colombian real estate attorney.

Can I explore the property remotely?

Yes. The interactive 3D site plan on this page is built on the land survey coordinates, and an advisor can walk you through the available lots over WhatsApp before you travel.

How do I schedule a tour?

Message us on WhatsApp at +57 310 401 7792 or use the form on this page, and a Núñez Anaya Inmobiliaria advisor will set up your site visit.
